    What's Next · In Development

    NAFPro AI Nexus

    The next layer above Atlas: an AI orchestration platform where QA engineers talk to their automation in natural language. Atlas stays the execution engine — Nexus becomes the intelligent brain.

    NAFPro AI Nexus

    Chat-driven execution

    A tester types "Run Product Config smoke tests" — Nexus recognises the intent, resolves the execution type, maps it to the correct suite in STATIM_Automation, launches it through NAFPro, tracks progress, and returns an analysed summary with suggested next actions. The full chain — natural language → planning → browser execution → report back — is already demonstrated end to end.

    Failure analysis on demand

    Ask "Why did Product Config fail?" and Nexus reads the automation reports, screenshots, RCA output, and Defect Writer draft — then explains the root cause, the failed step, the likely owner, and the probable fix, and offers to re-execute failed scenarios only. No more digging through logs manually.

    Enterprise-safe AI

    Built for finance and insurance-grade governance: Nexus runs entirely inside our network, automation executes locally, and reports stay on-premise. Passwords, PII, policy numbers, and financial values can be masked before anything reaches AI analysis. The LLM itself is swappable — OpenAI, Azure OpenAI, Ollama, or enterprise-hosted — so there is no vendor lock-in.

    Built, not vapour

    The Spring Boot AI platform, capability routing, execution planner, process engine, and multi-repository management are complete, reusing NAFPro's reporting, RCA, Defect Writer, and recovery engines without modification. Next phases: report intelligence, conversational analysis, AI authoring of scenarios and step definitions, and smart reruns.

    NAFPro → Atlas → Nexus. The framework keeps evolving — and it all stays in-house.
