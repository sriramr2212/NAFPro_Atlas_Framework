Two Approaches

Manual vs Automation

Manual

A Human Checks

Best for new features and judgment calls — "does this actually feel right to use?"

Automation

A Script Checks

Best for repeat checks — the same steps, hundreds of times, overnight, without complaint.

Testing login on Chrome, Edge, and Firefox across 4 screen sizes is 12 manual runs — or one automated suite that runs while you sleep.
The Trade-off

Why We Automate

Regression Risk

Old Things Break Silently

Every new feature can quietly damage something that already worked.

Human Limits

People Get Slow and Bored

Repeating the same 50 steps every week wears testers down. Scripts don't tire.

But automation can't say "this feels confusing." For judgment, taste, and new experiences — humans still matter most.

Part 2 · AI in Software Projects

AI Isn't Just in Testing Anymore

It's becoming a teammate across the whole project — not a separate department bolted on at the end.

Think of it as a very fast junior teammate. Fast, tireless, works at 2 a.m. — but still needs a senior to check its work.
